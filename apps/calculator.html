计算，还能检查依据

任意精度整数、十进制与有理数，支持标准、科学、分数、程序员、转换和 RPN 模式。审计页查看步骤、精度与舍入信息；数值近似结果不冒充精确值。

拍下来，核对后再算

Widget、控制中心控件、App Intents 与快捷指令、分享扩展和计算器键盘，让计算融入系统。键盘不要求“完全访问”；控制中心控件需要 iOS / iPadOS 18 或更高版本。

Calculator · iPhone & iPad

A precision workspace, not just a keypad.

Keep expressions, results and calculation details together. Use arbitrary-precision integers, decimals and rational numbers across standard, scientific, fraction, programmer, conversion and RPN modes. Inspect precision and rounding details; approximate numerical results remain identified as approximations.

Build reusable worksheets with variables and dependent expressions, organize documents with folders and tags, and import or export your work. The toolbox includes units, reference exchange rates, dates, loans, compound interest, statistics, regression, matrices, equations, plots and saved formulas.

On-device OCR extracts text from the camera, selected images or the first page of a PDF. Review and correct the text before calculating. This is not a conversational AI or an unrestricted word-problem solver.

Six complete skins apply across the app and its shared extension palette. Widgets, App Intents, Shortcuts, sharing and a keyboard that does not request Full Access fit your workflow. Control Center controls require iOS / iPadOS 18+. The app supports iOS / iPadOS 17+, Simplified Chinese and English.
