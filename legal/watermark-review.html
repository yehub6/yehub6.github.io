3. Functions, target audience, problem, and value

Watermark Camera is a Simplified Chinese, local photo-and-video watermarking camera for users who need organized visual records, including field workers, property and service staff, construction and inspection personnel, delivery personnel, travelers, and general users.

Users can take photos, press and hold to record videos with audio, or import existing photos and videos, then add date, time, weekday, coordinates, detailed address, nearby place name, device model, and custom text. Watermark color and corner position are editable. The local works gallery supports photo preview, video playback, editing, saving, sharing, and deleting.

Saving a watermark edit updates the current local work instead of adding a duplicate. Original media is retained locally inside the app, so later edits do not stack a previous watermark. Media, generated works, and settings are stored locally on the device. The app is a record-management utility; it does not provide tamper-proofing, electronic signatures, or legal certification.

4. Setup and access instructions

No account, login credentials, demo account, subscription, purchase, special configuration, or sample file is required.

  1. Launch the app and tap Shoot (拍摄) on the Home screen.
  2. Allow Camera access. To test detailed-address and nearby-place watermarks, allow Location While Using the App and enable Precise Location.
  3. Tap the shutter button to take a photo, or press and hold it to record a video. Allow Microphone access to include audio. Recorded video is previewed before the reviewer chooses Re-record (重录) or Save to Photos (保存到相册).
  4. Return to Home and tap Import (导入), then choose Import Photo (导入照片) or Import Video (导入视频) and select one item through the system picker.
  5. Open Gallery (相册) to preview or play, edit, share, save, or delete local works. Saving a watermark edit updates the currently opened work.
  6. Open Settings (设置) to change watermark content, style, grid, manage applicable advertising privacy choices, voluntarily watch a rewarded ad for an ad-free current session, share or rate the app, and access User Feedback.

If Location access is denied, the camera, importing, and all non-location watermark features remain available. If Microphone access is denied, recording remains available without audio. The system picker exposes only the photo or video explicitly selected by the reviewer.

5. External services, tools, and platforms

The app uses Apple-provided frameworks and system services, including App Tracking Transparency, plus Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform. It has no account system, payment or subscription service, cloud media storage, AI service, or developer-operated media/location backend.

Apple-provided frameworks and system services include:

  • Apple AVFoundation for camera, audio, video capture, playback, and local export
  • Apple Core Location for the current coordinates
  • Apple CLGeocoder and MapKit local search for detailed-address and nearby-place results
  • Apple PhotosUI and PhotoKit for user-controlled photo/video selection and saving results
  • Apple UIKit system share sheet for user-initiated sharing
  • Apple MessageUI or the user's installed Mail app for optional feedback
  • Apple StoreKit for the system App Store rating request

Google Mobile Ads SDK supplies app-open, native, banner, interstitial, rewarded, and rewarded-interstitial advertising. Google User Messaging Platform manages consent and advertising privacy choices where applicable. UMP does not replace Apple's ATT permission. The ATT decision is resolved before the Mobile Ads SDK starts or requests an ad; Google can access IDFA only when tracking is authorized. Google advertising services may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostics according to the user's choices and Google's policies.

All watermark composition and photo/video editing are performed locally. Location queries needed for detailed addresses and nearby places are handled by Apple system services; the developer does not receive them and operates no media or location server.

6. Regional differences

The app's core functions are available consistently in all regions, with no region-locked features or paid content. The current interface is Simplified Chinese. Ad consent and privacy options may vary by applicable law and Google UMP requirements. Ad availability can vary by region and network conditions. Address language and nearby-place detail can vary according to Apple Maps coverage, local map data, device language, and location accuracy.

If a detailed place name is unavailable, users can still use date, time, weekday, coordinates, device information, and custom-text watermarks.

7. Regulated industry and protected third-party material

The app is a general camera and photo/video-editing utility. It does not provide medical, financial, legal, gambling, transportation-booking, or government services. It does not bundle, sell, stream, or publish protected third-party material.

The app processes only photos and videos captured by the user or explicitly selected by the user through the system picker. Watermarks are not represented as tamper-proof records, electronic signatures, or legal certification. No regulatory authorization, third-party media license, demo account, or external credential is required.
