3. Functions, target audience, problem, and value

Photo Maker is a local photo utility for users who need portraits in common document, school, employment, resume, travel, application, or visa-photo proportions.

Apple Vision identifies the person and face on the device. The app removes or replaces the background, frames the portrait, and creates PNG, JPEG, or PDF output using preset or custom dimensions. It also supports common print-sheet layouts. Results can be saved to Photos or Files, or shared through the system sharing interface.

The app provides a quick way to prepare a clean, correctly proportioned portrait without sending the user's photo to an external server.

4. Setup and access instructions

No account, login credentials, subscription, purchase, activation, special configuration, or sample file is required. Any clear, front-facing portrait may be used. On the advertising-enabled iOS build, the reviewer may first see a Google UMP privacy form where required and Apple's ATT prompt. After the privacy flow, the app shows a branded loading screen while requesting an app-open ad. The ad can be closed to enter the main interface. If no ad is available, the app continues automatically.

  1. Launch the app. On iPhone or iPad, complete any UMP/ATT prompt, close the app-open ad if one is returned, and tap Choose Photo (选择照片). On Mac, click Open Photo (打开照片), press Command-O, or drag one image into the window.
  2. Select one clear, front-facing portrait.
  3. Wait for the on-device person-recognition process to finish.
  4. Select a preset size, or enter width and height under Custom Size (自定义尺寸), then choose a background color.
  5. Select Generate ID Photo (生成证件照), then choose PNG, JPEG, or PDF and an optional print layout.
  6. Select Save to Photos (保存到照片), Save to File (保存到文件), or Share with Other Apps (分享给其他应用).

The iOS system photo picker provides only the image explicitly selected by the user. On Mac, files are accessed only after the user selects or drops them. Camera permission is requested only when the user chooses direct capture. Photos permission is requested only when the user saves a result to Photos. The iOS/iPadOS advertising build may request App Tracking Transparency permission for access to IDFA. Denying ATT does not restrict any photo feature; ads may still be requested without IDFA. The app does not request microphone, location, or contacts.

5. External services, tools, and platforms

The iOS and iPadOS advertising build uses Google Mobile Ads SDK for app-open, banner, and native ads, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable privacy choices. Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, performance data, and non-user-related crash diagnostics as described in Google's iOS data-disclosure documentation. User-selected photos and Vision results are not sent to Google or the developer. The macOS build does not include Google Mobile Ads or UMP.

The app also uses these Apple system services and frameworks:

  • Apple Vision for on-device person and face detection
  • Apple PhotosUI and AppKit file panels for user-controlled photo selection
  • Apple Photos for saving a generated result
  • Apple UIKit or AppKit for the system sharing interface
  • Apple AVFoundation for optional Mac camera capture
  • Apple StoreKit for the system App Store review request
  • Apple AppTrackingTransparency in the iOS/iPadOS advertising build
  • The system Mail app for optional user feedback

6. Regional differences

The photo-editing functions are consistent in all available regions. Advertising and privacy messages can differ based on network availability, ad inventory, device settings, the user's choices, and regional requirements. UMP requests updated consent information at launch and ads are initialized only when UMP allows ad requests. The app supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.

7. Regulated industry and protected third-party material

Photo Maker is a general photo-editing utility. It does not issue identification, verify identity, submit applications to government authorities, or claim government affiliation. Preset names are photo-size references only, and users are advised to confirm the receiving organization's current requirements.

The app does not contain protected third-party material. No regulatory authorization, third-party license, demo account, or external credential is required.
