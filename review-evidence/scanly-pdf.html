简体中文

功能与实现

S12 中 Apple Vision 直接处理传入图像。D04 列出扫描、工具与纸面签署入口，属于本机既有说明，不证明这份文字曾随 Build 提交。纸面签署是文档编辑，不是身份认证、官方印章服务或法律效力认证。

联网用途与边界

本机说明涉及 Google 广告、用户选择的 iCloud 文档同步与 Apple 翻译资源下载。系统文件提供方也可能下载用户选择的云端文件。本机 OCR 不代表整个 App 没有网络。

复现步骤（尚待对应构建录屏）

  1. 正常启动，导入自有 PDF 或扫描不敏感样本。
  2. 执行 OCR、页面编辑、纸面签署、最终预览及导出。
  3. 展示工具与同步设置、广告／权限选择和语言资源状态。

待补充证据

具体版本、Build、实际提交备注、隐私申报及对应普通安装录屏仍待补充。

English

Functionality and implementation

S12 uses Apple Vision directly on the supplied image. D04 identifies scanning, Tools and Paper Signing as existing local notes, not proof those notes accompanied a Build. Paper Signing is document editing, not identity verification, an official seal service or certification of legal validity.

Network purposes and boundaries

Local notes disclose Google ads, optional iCloud Documents sync and Apple translation-resource downloads. System file providers may download selected cloud files. On-device OCR does not make the entire app network-free.

Reproduction steps (Build-matched recordings still required)

  1. Launch normally and import an owned PDF or scan a non-sensitive sample.
  2. Perform OCR, page editing, Paper Signing, Final Preview and export.
  3. Show Tools/sync settings, advertising/permission choices and language-resource state.

Evidence still required

The version, Build, actual submitted notes/privacy declarations and normal-install recordings remain to be supplied.
