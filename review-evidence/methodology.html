Evidence scope and public-edition handling

These materials record selected local source and existing local documents, not a verified match to binaries received by Apple. Distribution-build recordings, backend submission records, runtime traffic captures and remediation are not represented as completed. Existing website showcase media is a product guide, not automatically evidence of a historical distribution Build.

S01–S18 are selected source excerpts. D01–D09 are existing local review/handoff documents, not proof that their text was submitted. They may include earlier development plans, ICP requests or unverified assertions; their claims are not independently certified by republishing them. Local test branches are retained and explained, not treated as a whole-repository absence finding.

Personal names, team values, earlier case references and contact details are visibly redacted where present. Original relative paths and line numbers are retained. Original app code and private evidence originals are unchanged. Signed documents, backend screenshots, subscription credentials and distribution keys are not included.

Original full-file SHA-256 identifies the captured local file, not its creation/submission time. PUBLIC-SHA256SUMS.txt identifies public downloads, not original files. These are different hashes for different objects.

The site uses its normal compressed publishing pipeline, without authentication or access control. The noindex directive is not confidentiality protection. Downloads remain readable, and the same static content is served to all visitors.
