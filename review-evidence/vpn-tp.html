简体中文

功能与实现

D02 将 App 描述为配置文本工具，而非开发者运营的 VPN 连接服务。S09 有普通设置页可见的样例入口；S05、S06 的测试参数可跳过广告配置、引导或载入样例。部分入口未受 DEBUG 包围，需要对应实际上传 Build，不能把局部源码当成整个发行二进制审计。

联网用途与边界

S07 直接向用户指定的 HTTPS 地址请求文本。S08 实现 DNS、TCP 与 TLS 检查；移动版另有 Google 广告。这些是对外网络活动，使用 Apple API 不代表请求目标是 Apple。S06 的保留示例域名与样例字符串不代表实际节点服务。

复现步骤（尚待对应构建录屏）

  1. 不带测试参数正常启动，打开设置中的本地样例。
  2. 展示本地编辑、校验及转换，再导入本人有权访问的 HTTPS 配置源。
  3. 主动执行诊断并说明目标；对录屏和网络记录中的真实令牌、私钥及节点凭据脱敏。

待补充证据

需核验实际签名 entitlement、所有嵌入扩展、普通启动行为与 1.0.2 Build。此材料不请求 VPN 或地区规则豁免。

English

Functionality and implementation

D02 describes a configuration-text utility rather than a developer-operated VPN connection service. S09 exposes samples in normal Settings; test arguments in S05–S06 can bypass ad configuration/onboarding or load samples. Some entries are not DEBUG-guarded and must be matched to the uploaded Build. Selected source is not a full distribution-binary audit.

Network purposes and boundaries

S07 downloads text directly from a user-selected HTTPS URL. S08 implements DNS, TCP and TLS checks; the mobile app also has Google advertising. These are internet operations: Apple APIs do not make their destinations Apple services. Reserved example domains and sample strings in S06 are not a working node service.

Reproduction steps (Build-matched recordings still required)

  1. Launch without test arguments and open local samples in Settings.
  2. Demonstrate local editing/validation/conversion, then import an authorized HTTPS configuration source.
  3. Initiate a diagnostic and identify its target; redact real tokens, private keys and node credentials in recordings and traffic logs.

Evidence still required

Still required: signed entitlements, all embedded extensions, normal-launch behavior and the 1.0.2 Build. This material does not request exemption from VPN or regional rules.
